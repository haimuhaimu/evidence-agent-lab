# Privacy

Evidence Agent Lab accepts no real records. Use only the synthetic entity fixtures and synthetic queries included with the repository.

Do not enter or contribute names, contact details, account identifiers, credentials, production metrics, private screenshots, or operational records. Synthetic fixtures must declare their provenance and must not be derived from a real person or organization.

The demo performs no network submission of a run. Review entries are saved only in the current browser's local storage, remain on that device, and are limited to the local review format. Clearing browser storage removes them.

Local reviews do not train a model, change the deterministic planner, trigger production action, send an external notification, measure causal uplift, or provide real calibration.
